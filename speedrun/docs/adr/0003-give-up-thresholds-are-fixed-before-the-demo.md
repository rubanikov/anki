# Give-up thresholds are set from statistics and frozen before the demo

Each score abstains until it clears an evidence threshold. Those thresholds are
chosen from what makes the number mean something, not from what makes the demo
look complete: Performance needs 20 held-out attempts because at n=20 the 95%
interval around a 60% rate is already ±21 points, and below that the interval is
wider than the quantity being measured. The distinct-topic requirement is a
fraction of the section's topic count — one third, so BB 3, CP 4, PS 4 — rather
than the absolute 8 it was, which demanded 89% of Bio/Biochem and 67% of
Psych/Soc and so meant something different in every section.

## Consequences

- Lighting up all three sections needs 60 held-out attempts through a spoken
  loop. That will not happen before the deadline, so **at most one section
  reports Performance and Readiness; the others abstain naming their shortfall.**
  This is accepted deliberately: it is the give-up rule visibly working on real
  data, which is the product's whole argument.
- The obvious late fix — dropping the attempt threshold so every section reports
  in the demo video — is the exact behaviour that fails the project outright.
  Recording the reasoning here so that at hour 20 it is a decision already made
  rather than one being made under pressure.
- Thresholds stay in the backend, so neither the desktop add-on nor the Android
  client can talk past them.
